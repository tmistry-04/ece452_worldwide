# App Ideas

## 1. Band/Instrument Helper

**Potential Features:**
- Web scraper to find sheet music for each instrument
- Band group accounts — each member has their own sheet music and individual practice pieces
- Customizable warmup routines
- Synced sheet music playback with your band, synced with Spotify
- Live feedback/insights such as changing tempo
- Built-in metronome and tuner

## 2. Interactive Recipe App / Cooking Helper

**Potential Features:**
- AI assistant — interactive cooking helper to answer questions
- AI recipe insights — ask an LLM questions about the recipe
- Timeline — interactive UI to cleanly display steps and instructions, including parallel operations
- Individual timers baked into the timeline, UI-activated
- Recipe sharing, creating, and personalizing
- Recipe tagging, categorizing, and stats to help with nutritional goals
- Rich labelling — required appliances, ingredient costs
- Ingredient-based recipe finder
- Alternative ingredient finder
- Fridge inventory management
  - Take pictures of groceries, auto-detect best before dates on pre-made products, pre-populate freshness for produce

**Potential AWS Integrations** *(easy to connect via AWS SDK)*
- **Amazon Rekognition** *(Google Lens for food)* — point your phone at the fridge and the app detects what ingredients you have
- **Amazon Polly** *(Siri-style voice)* — hands covered in flour? The app reads instructions aloud
