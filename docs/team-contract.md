# Team Contract

## Roles

- Yuna is our team lead, and is responsible for coordinating the project.
- All other members will adapt to required roles as the project develops
- Specific roles may be assigned later

## Communication

- Our team will use Discord group chat to communicate project-related matters.
- We will meet every Tuesday at 11am in E7 (room to be determined).

## Meetings

- All members will attend these meetings or notify the team in advance of anticipated absences. We all agree that absences should be rare.
- All group members will come to the meetings prepared by:
  - reading the assigned material (as much as possible), and
  - coming with ideas pertaining to the tasks and decisions to be made.
  - adding their talking points to the agenda prior to the meeting.
- Members will not work on other assignments during meetings and will be engaged.
- Decisions can be made during meetings even if some members are absent, but opinions/feedback of all members will be solicited where possible.
- Each member will take turns listening as well as talking, and active listening will be a strategy for all group discussions.

- We will post the agenda for the week in our group chat each Monday.
- Team members will rotate taking notes during all meetings.
