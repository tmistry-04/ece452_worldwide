# Team Worldwide

Pantry Party is a fridge/food inventory management app that helps users track and manage the food in their house to reduce food waste, save time and save money. A report released by the food rescue organization Second Harvest revealed that 46% of food in Canada is wasted, 41% of which is avoidable, with an estimated value of $58 billion. Current options on the market/app stores include KitchenPal and NoWaste but are limited by subscription plans, inaccurate barcode/grocery scanning, and uninformative categorization of quantities (e.g., 100g vs 3 chicken thighs). Team Worldwide aims to rectify these shortcomings by creating an app that is free, reliable, convenient, and user-friendly.
